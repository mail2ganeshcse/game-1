kajakaja Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 6, 2026

kajakaja is an Android arcade runner game published under the package name com.kajakaja.game. This policy explains how the game handles user and device data.

Data Collection

kajakaja does not ask users to create an account and does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, photos, messages, health data, financial data, or other personal information.

Device Data

kajakaja does not intentionally collect, store, sell, or share sensitive user or device data. The game runs locally on the device and uses touch input only for gameplay controls.

Children

kajakaja is a general arcade game. If the target audience selected in Google Play includes children, the game is designed to avoid collecting personal information from children.

Advertising, Analytics, and Third Parties

kajakaja currently does not include advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, social login, payment processing, or third-party data-sharing features.

Health Information

kajakaja is not a health app. It does not provide medical, health, fitness, wellness, diagnostic, treatment, health-tracking, or health-data collection functionality.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated when the game changes. Updated versions will be posted on this page.

Contact

For privacy questions about kajakaja, contact the developer through the support contact listed on the Google Play Store listing.
